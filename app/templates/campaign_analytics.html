How to Read These Metrics

Delivered means SES received a delivery confirmation from the receiving mail server.

Bounces and complaints matter most for sender reputation. Your app should suppress contacts after a bounce or complaint.

Open rate is helpful but imperfect because Apple Mail Privacy Protection, Gmail image blocking, and other privacy tools can inflate or hide opens.

Click rate only appears when SES click tracking is active and the email contains trackable links.
