How to Read These Metrics

Most important for deliverability
Watch bounces and complaints first. High bounce or complaint rates can hurt sender reputation. Contacts who bounce or complain should be automatically suppressed by the webhook.
Opens are helpful but imperfect
Open tracking depends on images/tracking pixels. Apple Mail privacy tools, image blocking, and security scanners can make open data incomplete or inflated.
Click rate is more useful than open rate
Clicks usually show stronger engagement than opens. To test clicks, include a real link in the email body.
Delivery does not always mean inbox
Delivered means the receiving mail server accepted the message. It does not guarantee inbox placement instead of spam.
